#ShiftCovered: Clock out. We've got the rest.

A six-week social media campaign to launch HOP, the concierge platform I built, to healthcare workers at its first partner hospital, and to the hospital leaders who decide to offer it as a staff benefit.

ClientHOP · Hudson Valley Concierge Service LLC
My roleStrategy, copy, creative direction, measurement
Length6 weeksTease → Launch → Sustain
ChannelsInstagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, Facebook
→Strategy

Healthcare workers don't need another app. They need their evening back.

Most "perks" marketing to clinicians talks about the product. This campaign talks about the hours around the shift: the commute home, the empty fridge, the school pickup nobody can cover. HOP shows up as the thing that takes those off the list.

"The shift ends at 7. The second shift (groceries, kids, the car) starts at 7:15."

Core insight · the logistics tax

Objectives

  • Awareness: make "#ShiftCovered" recognizable among staff at the pilot hospital.
  • Activation: turn invited staff into members who submit their first request.
  • Pipeline: get HR and benefits leaders at nearby hospitals to book a HOP demo.
  • Trust: show that HOP is private and run by real people.

Big idea

#ShiftCovered borrows the language staff already use ("can you cover my shift?") and gives it to the off-hours. Every piece of content ends the same way: a nurse clocks out, and something on their list is already handled.

Signature tactic: post at shift change

Hospital shifts turn over around 7 AM and 7 PM. Posts go live at 7:45, after handoff, when staff are on break or heading out and finally checking their phones. The same posts at noon would reach people who are asleep or on the floor.

→Content pillars

Four kinds of posts, in a set mix.

40% of posts

Shift Stories

Real staff, with their consent, telling what HOP handled for them after a long shift.

"I got off at 7:30 and my car was already back from the shop."

25% of posts

The Logistics Tax

Carousels and quick explainers that name the hidden second shift and its cost.

"5 things you put off every week because you work 12s."

20% of posts

Behind the Concierge

The HOP team, the dispatch board, a request going from submitted to completed in real time.

"Meet the person who picked up your prescription."

15% of posts

Care Is a Feature

Plain-language posts on privacy: location sharing that asks first, wellness check-ins nobody scores.

"We don't track you. Here's exactly what we see."

✦Six-week plan

Tease, launch, then make it a habit.

Weeks 1–2 · Tease

Name the problem

No product yet. Just the logistics tax, said out loud.

  • "What's on your list after a 12?" question stickers
  • Logistics Tax carousel series
  • Teaser Reel: a clock hitting 7:00 PM, then "#ShiftCovered · soon"
Weeks 3–4 · Launch

Introduce HOP

Launch day timed to the 7:45 AM shift change at the pilot hospital.

  • Launch Reel and TikTok: one request, start to finish
  • Break-room QR posters linking to the invite flow
  • LinkedIn launch post plus a one-page benefits explainer
Weeks 5–6 · Sustain

Turn members into the story

Shift Stories from the first members, with written consent.

  • Weekly "Shift Covered" spotlight
  • Rewards-points nudge for a first request
  • LinkedIn data card: requests handled, hours given back

→Guardrails

Care is a feature in the campaign too.

No patient information, ever

No patients, charts, screens or identifiable hospital spaces in any shot. Staff content is reviewed before posting.

Written consent for every story

Shift Stories use signed release forms, and staff can pull their post at any time.

Hospital brand approval

The partner hospital's name and logo appear only after their communications team signs off.

Honest claims only

No invented testimonials and no "saves X hours" claims until real HOP data backs them up.
