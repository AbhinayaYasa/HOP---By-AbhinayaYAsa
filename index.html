→Featured work · 01

HOP · Hospitality On-Site Professionals

A login-gated concierge product a hospital can offer its staff, plus the public marketing site for the HOP brand. Built for Hudson Valley Concierge Service LLC. I own the codebase end to end.

The problem

Clinicians and nurses carry a "logistics tax" on top of their shifts: getting home, feeding the family, covering childcare, finding time for their own health. HOP gives them one place to ask for anything, with a real concierge team behind it.

What I built

  • Auth from scratchEmail and password with bcrypt, server sessions, single-use reset links that sign you out everywhere.
  • Dispatch workflowAssignment, a staff-only status lifecycle, full status history and dispatch notes.
  • Live ride locationConsent-first browser location sharing that stops the moment a ride leaves en_route.
  • Google CalendarReal OAuth connection so members see their upcoming shifts and events on the dashboard.
  • Wellness check-insPrivate, voluntary self-reports with a one-tap "request support now" path. No scoring, no surveillance.
  • ConciergeHubA separate staff portal for concierges and admins, with messaging, ratings and duty status.
  • Engagement layerMember feed, rewards points ledger, family profiles and a facility portal for hospital admins.
  • Email, AI and iOSTransactional email with Resend, a Claude-powered site assistant, and an iOS build with Capacitor.

Working inside a constraint

The client chose to stay on Vercel's Hobby plan, which caps each project at 12 serverless functions. Instead of asking for an upgrade, I consolidated endpoints behind ?action= dispatch so the whole platform, member app and staff portal, fits under the cap.

→How I work

Three habits that show up in everything I make.

Design around the constraint

A function cap, a one-person team, a shared database across two apps, a campaign with no ad budget to waste. I treat limits as the spec and build patterns that respect them.

Write down the why

Living architecture and scope docs record what is real, what is stubbed and what was tried and reversed, so nobody re-proposes a dead idea.

Care is a feature

Wellness data stays private, location sharing asks first and stops on its own, and campaign content never touches patient information. Trust is part of the product.
